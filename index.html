Nightshift is a detailing and wrap studio for cars and motorcycles. We wash by hand, polish the paint flat, coat it, or change its colour.

Clean itBook a slot

Hand wash. Soft mitts only.

Snow foam lifts the grit before anything touches the paint. Then two buckets, and the wheels, arches and door shuts each get their own.

Then we polish the paint flat.

Swirls and wash marks sit in the top of the clear coat. We machine polish them out in stages, until the ceiling lights reflect as straight lines.

Bikes get the same bench.

Fairings, tank, wheels, the chain side. Same process, smaller brushes.

Ceramic coating keeps it there.

A hard, slick layer over the corrected paint and the lights. Water beads and runs off, dirt has less to hold on to, and washing gets quicker.

Or change the colour.

Same car. Same bike. New colour.

A full wrap covers every painted panel in vinyl, and peels off cleanly the day you want your paint back. Pick one and see it wrapped.
